# EdTech & Online Learning — 10 Rules (index)

Source: Richard Mayer, *The Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning* (Cambridge University Press); Brown, Roediger, and McDaniel, *Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning* (Harvard University Press, 2014); CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 (udlguidelines.cast.org); ADL xAPI / SCORM standards; IMS Global LTI; WCAG 2.2 (W3C); FERPA (U.S. Dept. of Education, studentprivacy.ed.gov); COPPA (FTC). Building software for EdTech means delivering pedagogically sound, legally compliant, accessible learning experiences at scale — across LMS platforms, K-12 institutions, and self-paced consumer products.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |
|---|------|-----------|----------------------------|
