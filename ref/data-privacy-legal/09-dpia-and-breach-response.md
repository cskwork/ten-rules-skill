# Rule 09 — Assess High-Risk Processing and Report Breaches Fast

> 고위험 처리는 사전에 평가하고, 유출은 72시간 안에 신고하라.
> Assess high-risk processing before you start, and report breaches within 72 hours.

## Why

GDPR Article 35 requires a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) before high-risk processing such as large-scale profiling or sensitive data; Article 33 requires notifying the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach. These are deadline-driven duties: a missed DPIA or a late breach notice is itself a violation, compounding the underlying harm.

## How to apply

- Screen new high-risk processing (profiling, sensitive data, large scale, new tech) for a DPIA.
- In the DPIA, identify risks to people and the measures that reduce them, before launch.
- Maintain a breach response plan with named owners and a 72-hour notification clock.
- Detect, contain, and assess severity quickly; document every breach even if not notifiable.
- Notify affected individuals without undue delay when the risk to them is high.

## Checklist

- [ ] High-risk processing is screened, and a DPIA is completed before launch.
- [ ] DPIAs record concrete risk-reduction measures, not just risk descriptions.
- [ ] A breach response plan with owners and the 72-hour clock exists and is rehearsed.
- [ ] All breaches are logged, whether or not they require notification.
- [ ] High-risk breaches trigger timely notice to affected individuals.
