# Rule 08 — Tell People Clearly What You Do with Their Data

> 무슨 데이터를 왜, 누구와, 얼마나 쓰는지 쉬운 말로 알려라.
> Explain in plain language what data you use, why, with whom, and for how long.

## Why

Transparency is part of GDPR Article 5's first principle and is detailed in Articles 13 and 14: people must be told, in clear and plain language, what happens to their data. Privacy by Design calls this "visibility and transparency." A notice nobody can understand is not transparency; opaque or buried disclosures undermine consent and draw regulator scrutiny.

## How to apply

- Write the privacy notice in plain language, not legal boilerplate; layer detail progressively.
- State the specific purposes, lawful basis, recipients, retention, and the user's rights.
- Disclose at or before collection, in context, not only on a separate policy page.
- Keep cookie and tracking banners honest: real choices, no dark patterns or pre-consent loading.
- Update the notice when processing changes and tell affected users.

## Checklist

- [ ] The notice names purposes, recipients, retention, and rights in plain language.
- [ ] Disclosure happens at the point of collection, in context.
- [ ] Consent and cookie banners offer genuine choices without dark patterns.
- [ ] No tracking or non-essential cookies fire before consent is given.
- [ ] The notice is versioned and users are told of material changes.
