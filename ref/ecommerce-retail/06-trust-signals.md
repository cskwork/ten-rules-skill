# Rule 06 — Surface Trust Signals at Every Moment of Purchase Doubt

> 신뢰는 구매 결정의 모든 순간에 눈에 보여야 한다.
> Buyers scan for reasons to abort; place trust evidence at every point where doubt is highest.

## Why

E-commerce carries inherent friction that physical retail does not: you cannot touch the product, you are handing over payment details to a stranger, and you may need to navigate a return if something goes wrong. Nielsen Norman Group usability research identifies trust gaps as one of the primary reasons users abandon product pages and checkout, not just forms that are too long. Trust signals must be contextual — a security badge near the payment field, a returns policy near the "Add to Cart" button, star ratings near the price — because users scan pages in F-patterns and may never reach a dedicated trust section. Steve Krug's principle in *Don't Make Me Think* applies directly: users who must hunt for reassurance will leave rather than hunt.

## How to apply

- Place return and refund policy summary (not a link to a full page) within one scroll of the "Add to Cart" button on the product page; "Free 30-day returns" next to the price outperforms a footer link.
- Display aggregate star rating (if it exists) in the product title area, not only in the reviews section; link it to the reviews anchor.
- Show security indicators (HTTPS padlock behavior is browser-native; add a brief "Secure checkout" label or recognized payment logos) near the payment step — not only in the footer.
- Use verified review platforms (verified purchase badges, third-party review sources) rather than anonymous testimonials; Nielsen Norman research shows users distrust suspiciously uniform ratings.
- Communicate shipping estimate prominently: "Order by 3 pm for delivery by Thursday" converts better than a generic "3–5 business days" because it makes the outcome concrete.
- Surface seller or brand credibility signals on marketplace-style storefronts: years in business, number of orders fulfilled, response time.

## Checklist

- [ ] Return / refund summary (not just a link) is visible within one scroll of "Add to Cart".
- [ ] Aggregate rating is displayed in the product header area when reviews exist.
- [ ] Shipping estimate is date-specific or day-count-specific, not vague.
- [ ] Payment security indicators appear on the checkout payment step.
- [ ] Reviews show verified purchase status or a third-party source badge.
