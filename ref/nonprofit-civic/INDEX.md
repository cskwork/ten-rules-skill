# Nonprofit & Civic — 10 Rules (index)

Source: Grounded in Charity Navigator's Accountability & Finance methodology (charitynavigator.org), Nielsen Norman Group's "Attracting Donors and Volunteers" report (116 guidelines), W3C WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA standards, GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679), ADA Title II final rule (DOJ, April 2024), Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standards, and UNDP Civic Tech guidance. Building sites and software for nonprofits and civic organizations means serving mission-driven entities that depend on donor trust, regulatory transparency, and equitable access — where a failed donation flow or a missed disclosure can directly cost lives or legal standing.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |
|---|------|-----------|----------------------------|
| 01 | `01-donor-trust-transparency.md` | Publish financials and ratings prominently | donation pages, homepage, about pages, charity ratings, IRS 990 display |
| 02 | `02-donation-form-conversion.md` | Single-step, friction-minimal giving flows | donation forms, payment processing, recurring gifts, campaign pages |
| 03 | `03-wcag-inclusive-access.md` | WCAG 2.1 AA as a baseline, not an option | any UI component, forms, media, navigation, new page builds |

| 05 | `05-impact-reporting-display.md` | Show outcomes, not just outputs, near the ask | impact pages, campaign updates, annual reports, donor dashboards |
| 06 | `06-recurring-giving-retention.md` | Protect and recover recurring donor relationships | subscription billing, failed-payment flows, donor portals, cancellations |

| 09 | `09-volunteer-civic-engagement.md` | Lower the action barrier for non-financial contributors | volunteer sign-up, petition flows, event registration, civic participation |
