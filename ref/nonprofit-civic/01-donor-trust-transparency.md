# Rule 01 — Donor Trust and Financial Transparency

> 재정 정보와 평가 등급을 기부 페이지 가까이에 공개하라
> Publish financials and third-party ratings within one click of every giving action.

## Why

Seventy-two percent of individual donors say a charity rating badge increases their likelihood of giving. Charity Navigator's four-beacon methodology (Accountability & Finance, Impact, Leadership, Culture) is the dominant trust signal in US fundraising; absence of a rating — or hiding it behind navigation — causes first-time donors to bounce. Nonprofits that spend less than 70% of expenses on programs are penalized in ratings, so financial ratios must be accurate in the UI, not aspirational. Regulatory bodies and grant funders also require that the IRS Form 990 be publicly accessible, making its display a legal as well as UX obligation.

## How to apply

- Display your Charity Navigator, GuideStar/Candid, or CharityWatch badge on the donation page and the homepage footer — not only on an "About" or "Reports" page.
- Link directly to your most recently filed IRS Form 990 (or equivalent filing in non-US jurisdictions) from a dedicated "Financials" page; a link to the 990 hosted on an external site (e.g., ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer) is sufficient.
- Show program expense ratio as a visible metric (e.g., "87 cents of every dollar funds programs") adjacent to donation CTAs.
- Maintain a donor privacy policy that explicitly states the organization will not sell or share donor data without explicit permission; link it from every data-collection form.
- Version-control financial disclosures so the displayed year matches the most recently filed document; automate a stale-content alert when the filing date is more than 13 months old.

## Checklist

- [ ] Charity Navigator or equivalent rating badge visible on the donate page
- [ ] IRS Form 990 (or jurisdiction equivalent) linked and accessible within one click from the homepage
- [ ] Program expense ratio stated as a plain-language percentage near the donation CTA
- [ ] Donor privacy policy linked from every form that collects personal data
- [ ] Stale financial disclosure alert fires when filing date exceeds 13 months
