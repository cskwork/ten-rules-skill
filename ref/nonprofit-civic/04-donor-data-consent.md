# Rule 04 — Donor Data Consent and Privacy

> 후원자의 데이터는 명시적 동의 없이 저장하거나 연락에 사용하지 마라
> Never store or contact a donor without explicit, granular, documented consent.

## Why

GDPR applies to any nonprofit worldwide that processes personal data of EU/EEA residents — including accepting online donations from Europe. Penalties reach 4% of annual revenue or EUR 20 million, whichever is greater. California's CCPA/CPRA creates parallel obligations for California residents. Beyond regulation, donors who feel their data was misused destroy organizational reputation through social channels; a single viral complaint about unsolicited fundraising calls can erase years of brand trust. Lawful basis for fundraising communications under GDPR is almost always consent (not legitimate interest), because donors have a reasonable expectation that giving once does not authorize unlimited future contact.

## How to apply

- Present separate, unchecked opt-in checkboxes for each communication type (email newsletters, direct mail, phone calls, third-party partner communications) at the point of donation; pre-checked boxes are invalid consent under GDPR.
- Record consent timestamps, IP address, form version, and specific consent text in your CRM at the moment of submission; this audit trail is your legal defense.
- Implement a self-service preference center accessible from every email footer so donors can adjust or withdraw consent without contacting staff.
- Apply data minimization: collect only the fields required to process the gift and issue a legal receipt; do not collect phone number or birth date unless operationally necessary.
- Provide a documented data deletion workflow: when a donor exercises the right to erasure, anonymize or delete personal fields in the CRM, email platform, and any integrated analytics tools within 30 days.
- If you transfer donor data to third-party processors (payment gateways, email platforms, analytics), document those transfers in your privacy policy and ensure processor agreements include data protection clauses.

## Checklist

- [ ] Consent checkboxes are unchecked by default and separated by communication type
- [ ] Consent record (timestamp, form version, IP) stored in CRM on submission
- [ ] Preference center linked from every transactional and marketing email
- [ ] Privacy policy names all third-party data processors
- [ ] Data deletion procedure documented and tested end-to-end
