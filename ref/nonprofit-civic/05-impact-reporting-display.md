# Rule 05 — Impact Reporting Display

> 산출물이 아닌 결과물을 보여주고, 기부 요청 가까이에 배치하라
> Show outcomes, not just outputs, and place them adjacent to every giving ask.

## Why

Donors distinguish between outputs (number of meals served, events held) and outcomes (children no longer going hungry, families with stable housing). Displaying only activity counts does not answer the donor's question: "Did my gift change anything?" Effective impact display closes the 90-day peak engagement window — the period after a donation when a supporter is most likely to give again or upgrade their gift. Aligning with GRI or logic-model frameworks also satisfies institutional funders and grant reporting requirements, which increasingly demand outcome evidence rather than activity tallies.

## How to apply

- Structure impact data using a logic model hierarchy: Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact. Display at minimum the Outcomes layer (measurable changes in beneficiaries' lives) alongside any financial ask.
- Anchor impact statistics to a specific time period and source: "In 2024, 94% of program participants reported improved food security (n=1,240, annual survey)." Unattributed statistics breed skepticism.
- Place impact metrics visually above or immediately adjacent to the donation amount selector — not on a separate "Impact" page that requires navigation.
- Provide a post-donation impact acknowledgment: send an automated email within 90 days showing what the donor's specific tier of giving funded, using concrete outcome language.
- Avoid vanity metrics (Facebook likes, website visitors) in donor-facing impact displays; use only mission-outcome metrics.
- For recurring donors, surface a cumulative impact dashboard showing the total funded over their giving history.

## Checklist

- [ ] Impact section uses outcome language (change in beneficiary condition), not only output counts
- [ ] Statistics include time period, sample size, and methodology reference
- [ ] Impact content visible on the donation page without a separate navigation step
- [ ] Automated post-donation impact email fires within 90 days of each gift
- [ ] Recurring donor dashboard shows cumulative impact in mission terms
