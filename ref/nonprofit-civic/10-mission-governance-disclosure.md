# Rule 10 — Mission and Governance Disclosure

> 이사회 구성과 사업비 비율을 웹사이트에서 숨기지 마라
> Board composition, conflict-of-interest policies, and program expense ratios must be discoverable on the website.

## Why

Institutional donors, government grant agencies, and sophisticated individual donors research governance before committing major gifts. Charity Navigator's Leadership & Adaptability beacon and GuideStar Platinum Seal both require evidence of board independence, conflict-of-interest policies, and documented succession planning. Hiding this information behind "contact us" walls or in PDFs not linked from the website signals either disorganization or active concealment — both interpretations damage credibility. For civic organizations that receive public funding, governance transparency is often a legal condition of the grant, not a best practice.

## How to apply

- Maintain a public "Leadership" or "About Us > Governance" page listing all current board members by name and primary professional affiliation; update it within 30 days of any board change.
- Publish the organization's conflict-of-interest policy as a downloadable document linked from the governance page; note the date it was last reviewed by the board.
- Display the program expense ratio (percentage of total expenses going to programs vs. overhead) in the site footer or on the "About" page, with a link to the IRS Form 990 or equivalent filing for verification.
- For US nonprofits, link to the charity's GuideStar/Candid profile and any state charity registration filings; for non-US organizations, link to equivalent regulatory filings in the applicable jurisdiction.
- If the organization has received any regulatory action, fines, or public complaints, disclose these on the governance page with context and resolution status — discovered concealment causes disproportionately greater reputational damage than disclosed issues.
- Keep governance content in version control or a CMS with audit history so changes (especially board removals) can be traced if challenged.

## Checklist

- [ ] Current board member list with professional affiliations published and updated within 30 days of changes
- [ ] Conflict-of-interest policy linked from the governance page with a last-reviewed date
- [ ] Program expense ratio displayed prominently with a link to the supporting filing
- [ ] IRS Form 990 or jurisdiction-equivalent linked from the financials or governance page
- [ ] Governance page content change history is auditable in the CMS or version control
