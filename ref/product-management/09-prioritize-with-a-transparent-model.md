# Rule 09 — Prioritize With a Transparent Model

> 우선순위는 목소리 크기가 아니라 드러난 점수로 정하라.
> Set priority by a visible score, not by who argues loudest.

## Why

Intercom's RICE model scores initiatives by Reach times Impact times Confidence, divided by Effort, yielding impact per unit of work. An explicit model replaces HiPPO-driven roadmaps and opinion battles with a comparable, auditable score. The Confidence term is the discipline that stops optimistic estimates from inflating low-evidence bets. The point is not the exact number but a consistent, transparent basis everyone can challenge.

## How to apply

- Score each initiative on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort consistently.
- Define Reach over a fixed time window and Effort in person-months.
- Use Confidence to discount bets that lack supporting evidence.
- Rank by score, then sanity-check against strategy before committing.
- Keep the scoring inputs visible so anyone can question an assumption.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every candidate is scored on all four RICE factors
- [ ] Reach uses a defined time window
- [ ] Confidence reflects actual evidence, not optimism
- [ ] Rankings are reviewed against strategy before commitment
- [ ] Scoring assumptions are documented and visible
