# Rule 04 — Talk to Customers Continuously

> 발견은 한 번의 조사가 아니라 매주 반복하는 습관이다.
> Discovery is a weekly habit, not a one-time research project.

## Why

Teresa Torres defines continuous discovery as weekly touchpoints with customers by the team building the product, not a quarterly study handed off by researchers. Insight decays and markets shift, so a single research sprint goes stale fast. Regular, small contact keeps the team's assumptions honest and surfaces opportunities before competitors. The discovery cadence is what makes outcome-driven work sustainable.

## How to apply

- Schedule a recurring weekly customer interview the whole trio attends.
- Interview about real past behavior and stories, not hypothetical preferences.
- Capture interview snapshots and feed them into a shared opportunity map.
- Recruit continuously so you never block on finding participants.
- Let recent interviews, not opinion, settle disagreements about user needs.

## Checklist

- [ ] A standing weekly customer touchpoint is on the calendar
- [ ] Product, design, and engineering all hear from customers directly
- [ ] Interviews probe specific past behavior, not opinions
- [ ] Insights are captured in a shared, searchable place
- [ ] A continuous recruiting pipeline exists for participants
