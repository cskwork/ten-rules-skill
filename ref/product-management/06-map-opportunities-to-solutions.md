# Rule 06 — Map Opportunities Before Solutions

> 해결책을 고르기 전에 기회의 지형을 그려라.
> Chart the landscape of opportunities before you pick a solution.

## Why

Teresa Torres's opportunity solution tree connects one outcome to the customer opportunities that drive it, and only then to candidate solutions and experiments. Jumping straight to solutions hides the assumption that you picked the right problem. The tree makes the team's thinking visible, exposes neglected opportunities, and forces a comparison of multiple solutions per opportunity instead of committing to the first idea that surfaced.

## How to apply

- Put the single target outcome at the root of the tree.
- Branch into distinct customer opportunities (unmet needs and pain points).
- Size and compare opportunities, then choose one to attack at a time.
- Generate at least three competing solutions per chosen opportunity.
- Attach experiments to each solution to test its underlying assumptions.

## Checklist

- [ ] A single outcome anchors the tree
- [ ] Opportunities are distinct customer needs, not solutions in disguise
- [ ] Opportunities are compared before one is selected
- [ ] Multiple solutions are generated for the chosen opportunity
- [ ] Each solution links to a concrete experiment
