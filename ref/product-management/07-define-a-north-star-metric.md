# Rule 07 — Define a North Star Metric and Its Inputs

> 모두가 같은 방향으로 노 젓게 하는 단 하나의 가치 지표를 정하라.
> Pick the one value metric that makes everyone row in the same direction.

## Why

Amplitude's North Star Framework centers a team on a single metric that captures the value customers get from the product, supported by three to five input metrics teams can directly influence. Without it, teams chase conflicting local metrics and ship features that look busy but create no value. A good North Star is a leading indicator of customer value and revenue, not a vanity count like raw signups or page views.

## How to apply

- Choose one North Star that measures realized customer value, not activity.
- Decompose it into three to five inputs teams can move through their work.
- Avoid vanity metrics (registrations, downloads) that lack value signal.
- Pair the North Star with a counter-metric to prevent gaming.
- Review the North Star and inputs on a regular cadence and revise as you learn.

## Checklist

- [ ] A single North Star metric is defined and reflects customer value
- [ ] Three to five input metrics decompose the North Star
- [ ] Each input is directly influenceable by a team's work
- [ ] A counter-metric guards against gaming
- [ ] The metric is reviewed on a set cadence
