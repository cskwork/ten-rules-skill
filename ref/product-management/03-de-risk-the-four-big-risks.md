# Rule 03 — De-Risk the Four Big Risks Before You Build

> 가치, 사용성, 실현 가능성, 사업성을 만들기 전에 먼저 검증하라.
> Test value, usability, feasibility, and viability before you commit engineering.

## Why

Marty Cagan and SVPG name four risks that actually kill products: value (will anyone want it), usability (can they use it), feasibility (can we build it), and viability (does the business support it). Discovery exists to attack all four before delivery starts. Teams that skip this validate their ideas in production, at the highest possible cost. Cheap experiments before code are the core of product discovery.

## How to apply

- For each idea, write the value, usability, feasibility, and viability risk explicitly.
- Rank the four by uncertainty and consequence; attack the riskiest first.
- Use the cheapest test that resolves each risk: interviews, prototypes, spikes, legal review.
- Bring engineering and business stakeholders into discovery, not just delivery.
- Decide go, pivot, or kill on evidence before scheduling build work.

## Checklist

- [ ] All four risks are documented for the initiative
- [ ] The highest-uncertainty risk is tested first
- [ ] A concrete experiment exists for each unresolved risk
- [ ] Feasibility checked with engineering and viability with the business
- [ ] A go/pivot/kill decision is recorded with evidence
