# Product Management — 10 Rules (index)

Source: market-researched synthesis grounded in Marty Cagan / SVPG, Teresa Torres (Continuous Discovery Habits), Jobs-to-be-Done (Christensen / Ulwick), Eric Ries (Lean Startup), and the Amplitude North Star and AARRR frameworks. Product management is the discipline of discovering and delivering products that create measurable customer and business value.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.
