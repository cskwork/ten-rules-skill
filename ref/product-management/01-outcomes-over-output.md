# Rule 01 — Pursue Outcomes, Not Output

> 만든 양이 아니라 바뀐 결과로 성공을 측정하라.
> Measure success by the change you create, not the features you ship.

## Why

Shipping is the cost, not the goal. Marty Cagan and SVPG define the modern product operating model around outcomes over output: a team is accountable for moving a customer or business result, not for completing a feature list. Teams that count releases optimize for activity and routinely ship things nobody adopts. Tying work to a measurable outcome is the single highest-leverage habit in product management.

## How to apply

- Frame every initiative as a target outcome (a metric change), never as a feature to build.
- Give teams a problem and a measurable goal; let them choose the solution.
- State the baseline and the target movement before work starts.
- Kill or pivot work that does not move the outcome, even if it is "done."
- Report progress as outcome delta, not as story points or features shipped.

## Checklist

- [ ] Each initiative names a specific metric and target change
- [ ] The current baseline for that metric is recorded
- [ ] Success criteria are written before building starts
- [ ] Roadmap items are phrased as outcomes, not feature names
- [ ] Shipped work without outcome movement is reviewed for pivot or kill
