# Rule 08 — Measure the Full Funnel

> 가입만 보지 말고 획득부터 추천까지 흐름 전체를 따라가라.
> Don't watch signups alone; follow the flow from acquisition to referral.

## Why

Dave McClure's Pirate Metrics (AARRR: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue) force teams to see the whole customer lifecycle, not just the top of the funnel. A product can win signups and still die at activation or retention. Measuring each stage isolates where value actually leaks, so you fix the real bottleneck instead of pouring more spend into a stage that is already healthy. Retention is usually the truest test of product-market fit.

## How to apply

- Instrument all five AARRR stages with a defined event for each.
- Find the largest drop-off stage and prioritize that bottleneck first.
- Treat retention as the core fit signal before scaling acquisition spend.
- Define activation as a concrete first-value moment, not just account creation.
- Segment the funnel by cohort and source to expose hidden differences.

## Checklist

- [ ] All five AARRR stages have defined, instrumented events
- [ ] The biggest drop-off stage is identified
- [ ] Activation is defined as a specific first-value moment
- [ ] Retention is tracked by cohort over time
- [ ] Funnel is segmented by acquisition source or cohort
