# Rule 04 — Validate Price Before You Build

> 만들기 전에 고객이 얼마를 낼지부터 확인하라.
> Find out what customers will pay before you build the product.

## Why

Madhavan Ramanujam's Monetizing Innovation argues the most common cause of new-product failure is treating price as an afterthought. Willingness to pay should be tested early, and the product designed around the price and the value customers actually reward. Price is the single strongest lever on profit per unit, so a few points of pricing discipline outweigh large cost-cutting efforts. Validate demand and willingness to pay before sinking engineering cost.

## How to apply

- Have the willingness-to-pay conversation with target customers during discovery, not after launch.
- Segment by value: different customers reward different benefits at different prices.
- Design packaging and tiers around the features customers will actually pay for.
- Choose a pricing model (per seat, usage, tiered, value metric) that scales with the value delivered.
- Test price points and bundles before committing the roadmap to them.

## Checklist

- [ ] Willingness to pay was tested with real prospects before build
- [ ] Pricing tiers map to distinct value segments
- [ ] The value metric scales with customer-perceived value
- [ ] Price was set deliberately, not as cost-plus markup
- [ ] Packaging is documented and revisited as the product evolves
