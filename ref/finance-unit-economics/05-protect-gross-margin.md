# Rule 05 — Protect and Report True Gross Margin

> 매출총이익률이 어떤 사업인지를 결정한다.
> Gross margin defines what kind of business you actually are.

## Why

Gross margin (revenue minus cost of goods sold, as a percent) sets the ceiling on every downstream metric: LTV, payback, and the value of each retention point all scale with it. Bessemer's cloud research treats high, durable gross margin as a marker of true software economics, guiding toward roughly 65 to 80 percent for software. A "software" company with 40 percent margins is really a services business, and its valuation and growth math change accordingly.

## How to apply

- Put all true cost of delivery into COGS: hosting, third-party APIs, payment fees, delivery support.
- Report gross margin honestly; do not move delivery costs into operating expense to inflate it.
- Distinguish gross margin (includes fixed delivery costs) from contribution margin (variable only).
- Track margin by product line and segment; blended margin hides low-margin drag.
- Drive margin up over time via automation, vendor leverage, and pricing, not by reclassifying costs.

## Checklist

- [ ] COGS includes all genuine cost of delivery
- [ ] Gross margin is reported consistently period to period
- [ ] Margin is benchmarked against the relevant business-model range
- [ ] Low-margin product lines are identified, not averaged away
- [ ] A plan exists to improve margin without reclassifying costs
