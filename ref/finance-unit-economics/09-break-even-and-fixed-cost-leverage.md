# Rule 09 — Find Break-Even and Fixed-Cost Leverage

> 손익분기점은 고정비를 단위당 공헌이익으로 나눈 값이다.
> Break-even is fixed costs divided by contribution margin per unit.

## Why

Break-even analysis answers how much you must sell to stop losing money: fixed costs divided by contribution margin per unit gives the break-even volume. It is a core managerial-accounting tool because it connects unit economics (Rule 01) to the whole business and exposes operating leverage. Once fixed costs are covered, each additional unit's contribution margin drops almost entirely to profit, so understanding this point clarifies pricing, volume, and cost decisions.

## How to apply

- Separate fixed costs (rent, salaries, baseline tooling) from variable costs cleanly.
- Compute break-even units as total fixed costs divided by contribution margin per unit.
- Compute break-even revenue using the contribution-margin ratio for service or mixed models.
- Model how price and cost changes shift the break-even point before committing to them.
- Track distance to break-even as a planning anchor alongside burn and runway.

## Checklist

- [ ] Fixed and variable costs are cleanly separated
- [ ] Break-even volume is calculated from contribution margin per unit
- [ ] Break-even revenue is known for mixed or service models
- [ ] Sensitivity to price and cost changes is modeled
- [ ] Current volume is compared against the break-even point
