# Finance and Unit Economics — 10 Rules (index)

Source: market-researched synthesis grounded in David Skok / forEntrepreneurs (SaaS metrics), Bessemer Venture Partners (cloud metrics), Madhavan Ramanujam (Monetizing Innovation), and classic managerial accounting (contribution margin, break-even). Unit economics is the study of the revenue and cost of one unit — one customer, order, or product — to prove a business or product can make money at scale, distinct from securities investing.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.
