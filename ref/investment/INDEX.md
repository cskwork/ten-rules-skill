# Investment — Ten Commandments (index)

Source: `github.com/cskwork/investment-agent-rules`. Capital preservation first, then value,
margin of safety, business quality, edge honesty (Graham/Buffett/Munger/Fisher/Lynch/Marks/Bogle/Malkiel).

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open `common/<file>` for the full reasoning + checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |
|---|------|-----------|----------------------------|
| 01 | `common/01-capital-preservation.md` | Never lose money — preserve capital first | risk sizing, downside, "should I sell" |
| 02 | `common/02-buy-value-not-price.md` | Buy value, not price | what something is worth vs. quoted |
| 03 | `common/03-margin-of-safety.md` | Demand a margin of safety | entry price, intrinsic value gap |
| 04 | `common/04-stock-is-a-business.md` | A stock is a piece of a business | thesis, fundamentals, holding period |
| 05 | `common/05-circle-of-competence.md` | Stay in your circle of competence | "do I understand this?" |
| 06 | `common/06-quality-at-fair-price.md` | Quality at a fair price beats cheap mediocrity | moat, returns on capital |
| 07 | `common/07-management-and-capital-allocation.md` | Verify management & capital allocation | governance, buybacks, dividends, debt |
| 08 | `common/08-long-term-compounding.md` | Compound long-term; don't interrupt it | turnover, taxes, patience |
| 09 | `common/09-cycles-and-crowd-psychology.md` | Use cycles & crowd psychology against the crowd | sentiment, bubbles, timing |
| 10 | `common/10-default-to-low-cost-diversification.md` | No edge → default to low-cost diversification | index vs. picking, fees |
