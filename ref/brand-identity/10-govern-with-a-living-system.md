# Rule 10 — Govern With a Living System

> 가이드라인은 박물관이 아니라 살아 있는 시스템이다.
> Guidelines are not a museum; they are a living system.

## Why

Marty Neumeier (The Brand Gap) stresses that a brand is sustained over time by people and process, not by a one-off style guide that ages on a shelf. Without living governance, the consistency that builds recognition (Ehrenberg-Bass) erodes as teams, vendors, and channels multiply. Brand equity is a managed asset (Aaker), and management means accessible assets, clear ownership, and review. Treat the brand system as a product that is maintained, not a document that is finished.

## How to apply

- Publish guidelines plus ready-to-use assets in one accessible, versioned source of truth.
- Assign clear brand ownership with authority to approve and to correct off-brand work.
- Onboard every new team member, agency, and partner to the system before they produce work.
- Review live executions on a schedule and feed gaps back into the guidelines.
- Version and changelog the system so evolution is deliberate and traceable.

## Checklist

- [ ] Guidelines and usable assets live in one accessible, versioned source
- [ ] A named owner has authority to approve and enforce brand decisions
- [ ] New contributors and partners are onboarded before producing work
- [ ] A recurring review feeds real-world gaps back into the system
