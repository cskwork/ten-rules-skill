# Rule 04 — Mental and Physical Availability

> 떠오르기 쉽고 사기 쉬운 브랜드가 성장한다.
> Brands grow by being easy to think of and easy to buy.

## Why

The Ehrenberg-Bass Institute's central finding (How Brands Grow) is that brands grow through two levers: mental availability (being easily recalled in buying situations) and physical availability (being easy to find and buy). Identity work serves the first by building memory structures, and it must support the second by being recognizable everywhere the brand is sold. A brand strong in only one lever loses sales it could have won. Design identity to be retrieved from memory and to stand out on the shelf.

## How to apply

- Identify the category entry points (the situations and needs) you want the brand recalled in.
- Build identity assets that link the brand to those entry points in customer memory.
- Ensure assets remain recognizable at small sizes, in motion, and in cluttered retail or feed contexts.
- Maximize reach: keep identity consistent across every channel where buyers encounter you.
- Reduce friction so that recognition converts to purchase wherever the brand appears.

## Checklist

- [ ] Key category entry points are documented and prioritized
- [ ] Identity assets link the brand to those entry points
- [ ] Assets stay recognizable when small, moving, or surrounded by clutter
- [ ] Identity is consistent across every point of sale and channel
