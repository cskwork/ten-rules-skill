# Rule 05 — Start With Why

> 사람들은 무엇을 하는지가 아니라 왜 하는지를 산다.
> People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it.

## Why

Simon Sinek (Start With Why) shows that durable brands lead with purpose: the why (belief), then how (method), then what (offering). A brand that communicates only features competes on commodity terms; a brand grounded in a clear why earns belief, loyalty, and a reason to choose it over equals. The why is the source of brand meaning that strategy, voice, and visuals then express. Define the belief before the product story.

## How to apply

- Articulate the why: the cause or belief that exists beyond making money.
- Define the how: the distinctive principles and methods that bring the why to life.
- State the what: the products and services as proof of the why, not as the lead message.
- Lead external messaging with the why so customers connect on belief, not specs.
- Check that internal culture and decisions reflect the stated why, or it rings hollow.

## Checklist

- [ ] A clear why (belief or cause) is written, distinct from revenue goals
- [ ] How and what are defined and trace back to the why
- [ ] Lead messaging communicates the why before the what
- [ ] Internal behavior is consistent with the stated why
