# Rule 02 — Earn Product/Market Fit Before You Scale

> 제품/시장 적합성 없이 성장 엔진을 밟으면 빠르게 망한다.
> Stepping on the growth pedal before product/market fit only crashes you faster.

## Why

Premature scaling is the most common cause of startup death; spend amplifies a product people do not yet need. Sean Ellis's must-have survey gives a concrete bar: ask "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" and aim for 40%+ answering "very disappointed." Below that threshold, fix the product and positioning; above it, growth becomes meaningfully easier. PMF is a prerequisite, not a phase you skip.

## How to apply

- Run the Sean Ellis survey on activated users; track the "very disappointed" percentage over time.
- Treat 40%+ as the green light to scale; below it, invest in product and segment focus.
- Segment survey results to find the audience for whom you are already must-have.
- Pair the survey with qualitative interviews to learn why users would be disappointed.
- Re-measure PMF after major product or audience changes; it is not permanent.

## Checklist

- [ ] Sean Ellis must-have survey is fielded to real activated users
- [ ] The "very disappointed" percentage is tracked against the 40% bar
- [ ] Scaling decisions are gated on PMF evidence, not optimism
- [ ] The high-fit segment is identified and prioritized
