# Rule 05 — Build Growth Loops, Not Just Funnels

> 깔때기는 위에서 계속 부어야 하지만, 루프는 출력이 다음 입력을 만든다.
> A funnel needs constant pouring at the top; a loop turns its output back into its own input.

## Why

Brian Balfour and the Reforge team argue that "growth loops are the new funnels": the fastest-growing products grow through self-reinforcing systems, not one-way funnels. A funnel has no mechanism to reinvest what comes out the bottom, so growth never compounds and you must keep buying the top. A loop is a closed cycle where the output of one cycle (new users, content, data, revenue) becomes the input that drives the next, creating compounding defensibility.

## How to apply

- Map your primary loop end to end: action, output, and how that output feeds the next input.
- Classify the loop type (viral/word-of-mouth, content, paid reinvestment) and measure its cycle time and amplification.
- Reduce loop cycle time and increase the conversion at each step to speed compounding.
- Still use funnels as a diagnostic lens inside a loop, but optimize the loop as the system.
- Avoid relying on a single fragile loop; understand which loops are durable for your model.

## Checklist

- [ ] At least one primary growth loop is mapped as a closed cycle
- [ ] Loop output is explicitly shown feeding the next input
- [ ] Loop cycle time and amplification factor are measured
- [ ] The team optimizes the loop system, not just isolated funnel steps
