# Rule 01 — Retention Is the Foundation of Growth

> 유지율이 무너지면 어떤 성장도 새는 양동이에 물 붓기다.
> If retention leaks, every other growth effort just pours water into a broken bucket.

## Why

Retention is the base layer that makes acquisition, referral, and revenue compound; without it growth is a leaky bucket. Reforge teaches that retention improvements have far larger valuation impact than equivalent CAC reductions, because a small churn decrease compounds into much higher LTV. Sean Ellis's product/market-fit work exists precisely because durable growth is impossible before users keep coming back. Fix retention before scaling spend.

## How to apply

- Plot retention curves by cohort; require the curve to flatten (stabilize) rather than decay to zero.
- Find your product's natural usage frequency, then measure retention on that cadence (daily, weekly, monthly).
- Define and instrument a "resurrection" path for churned users before chasing new ones.
- Treat a flattening retention curve as the gate to scale paid acquisition.
- Diagnose churn by segment and lifecycle stage, not as one aggregate number.

## Checklist

- [ ] Cohort retention curves exist and the curve flattens, not decays to zero
- [ ] Retention is measured at the product's natural usage frequency
- [ ] Acquisition scale-up is gated on a stable retention curve
- [ ] Churn is broken down by cohort and lifecycle stage
