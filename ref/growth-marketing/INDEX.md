# Growth Marketing — 10 Rules (index)

Source: market-researched synthesis grounded in April Dunford (positioning), Sean Ellis (product/market fit + ICE), Brian Balfour / Reforge (growth loops, four fits), and Dave McClure (AARRR pirate metrics). Growth marketing is the discipline of building repeatable, measurable, compounding systems that acquire, activate, retain, and monetize customers profitably.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |

| 02 | `02-earn-product-market-fit-first.md` | Reach product/market fit before scaling spend | PMF, must-have survey, early traction |
| 03 | `03-position-deliberately.md` | Choose your market frame on purpose | positioning, category, competitive framing |
| 04 | `04-message-the-job.md` | Message the job to be done, not features | messaging, copy, value proposition |
