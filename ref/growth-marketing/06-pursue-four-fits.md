# Rule 06 — Pursue Channel-Model-Product Fit

> 제품, 채널, 수익 모델을 따로 설계하면 분배는 거의 항상 실패한다.
> Design product, channel, and revenue model in silos and distribution almost always fails.

## Why

Brian Balfour's Four Fits framework shows that growth fails when teams treat product, channel, and monetization as separate problems. Channels have constraints, so products must be molded to fit the channel, not the reverse; and the monetization model enables or disables certain channels (channel-model fit). Picking a channel that does not match how your product is discovered or how it makes money wastes the entire acquisition budget. The fits must reinforce each other.

## How to apply

- Pick a small number of channels whose mechanics match how your product is naturally discovered and shared.
- Check channel-model fit: a low-ACV product cannot afford a high-touch sales channel; a high-ACV product needs one.
- Mold the product (onboarding, virality hooks, packaging) to the chosen channel's constraints.
- Concentrate effort: master one or two channels before diversifying, rather than spreading thin.
- Revisit the fits whenever you change pricing, audience, or product, since they are interdependent.

## Checklist

- [ ] Channels are chosen to match product discovery and sharing mechanics
- [ ] Channel-model fit is validated against price point and margins
- [ ] The product is adapted to fit channel constraints
- [ ] Focus is concentrated on one or two channels before expanding
