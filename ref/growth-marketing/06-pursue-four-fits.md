# Rule 06 — Pursue the Four Fits Together

> 제품, 채널, 수익 모델을 따로 설계하면 분배는 거의 항상 실패한다.
> Design product, channel, and revenue model in silos and distribution almost always fails.

## Why

Brian Balfour's Four Fits framework names four interdependent fits that must align for sustainable growth: Market-Product Fit (the market demands what you built), Product-Channel Fit (the product's mechanics match how the channel works), Channel-Model Fit (the revenue model can afford the channel's economics), and Model-Market Fit (the pricing model fits the market's willingness to pay and size). Growth fails when any one fit is missing; they must reinforce each other as a system, not be solved independently.

## How to apply

- Confirm Market-Product Fit before optimizing any other fit: the market must demonstrably want what you built.
- Pick a small number of channels whose mechanics match how your product is naturally discovered and shared (Product-Channel Fit).
- Check Channel-Model Fit: a low-ACV product cannot afford a high-touch sales channel; a high-ACV product needs one.
- Validate Model-Market Fit: ensure your pricing model and deal size match the market segment's willingness to pay and TAM.
- Mold the product (onboarding, virality hooks, packaging) to the chosen channel's constraints.
- Revisit all four fits whenever you change pricing, audience, or product, since they are interdependent.

## Checklist

- [ ] Market-Product Fit is validated before scaling any other fit
- [ ] Channels are chosen to match product discovery and sharing mechanics (Product-Channel Fit)
- [ ] Channel-model fit is validated against price point and margins
- [ ] Model-Market Fit is confirmed: pricing and deal size match the target segment
- [ ] Focus is concentrated on one or two channels before expanding
