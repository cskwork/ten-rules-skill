# Rule 08 — Engineer Activation to the Aha Moment

> 가입은 시작일 뿐이다 — 사용자가 핵심 가치를 처음 경험하게 만들어라.
> Signup is only the start; activation is getting the user to first real value.

## Why

Activation, the second stage of AARRR, is where curiosity converts into experienced value, and it is the strongest leading indicator of retention. Product-led growth depends on users reaching the "aha moment" (the action correlated with long-term retention) as fast as possible. Facebook's "7 friends in 10 days" is the classic example of an activation metric tied to a setup milestone. Weak activation makes every dollar of acquisition decay before retention can take hold.

## How to apply

- Identify the setup action that statistically predicts retention; make that your activation metric.
- Strip onboarding to the shortest path to first value; remove every non-essential step.
- Reduce time-to-value with templates, defaults, sample data, and guided first runs.
- Measure activation rate and time-to-activate as core funnel KPIs, by cohort.
- Run targeted onboarding experiments on the steps where most new users drop off.

## Checklist

- [ ] An activation/aha metric tied to retention is defined
- [ ] Onboarding is the shortest viable path to first value
- [ ] Activation rate and time-to-value are measured by cohort
- [ ] Drop-off steps in onboarding are identified and tested
