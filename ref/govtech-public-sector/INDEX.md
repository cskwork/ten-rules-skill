# GovTech & Public Sector — 10 Rules (index)

Source: Grounded in the GOV.UK Service Standard (CDDO, service-manual.gov.uk), the US Digital Services Playbook (USDS, playbook.usds.gov), NIST SP 800-63-4 Digital Identity Guidelines, Section 508 / WCAG 2.2 accessibility law, the Plain Writing Act of 2010 (Federal Plain Language Guidelines), and the W3C DCAT-US v3 open data standard. Building for government means delivering services that citizens depend on for benefits, safety, and legal obligations — where failure is not a product inconvenience but a public harm.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |
|---|------|-----------|----------------------------|
| 01 | `01-user-need-over-organisational-convenience.md` | Serve the user's actual need, not internal process | Any new feature, form, or service flow |
| 02 | `02-accessibility-as-legal-baseline.md` | Accessibility is law, not a stretch goal | Any UI component, page, document, or PDF |
| 03 | `03-plain-language-content.md` | Write at a reading level citizens can act on | Any user-facing copy, label, error, or notification |
| 04 | `04-digital-identity-assurance.md` | Match identity proofing to the risk of the transaction | Any login, account creation, or credentialing flow |
| 05 | `05-open-by-default-data.md` | Publish structured, licensed open data by default | Any dataset, API, or data pipeline exposed externally |
| 06 | `06-design-for-assisted-digital.md` | Support users who cannot use digital channels alone | Any end-to-end service that replaces an offline process |
| 07 | `07-incremental-delivery-in-the-open.md` | Ship in small iterations; show and tell publicly | Any product phase gate, alpha/beta/live decision |
| 08 | `08-technology-ownership-and-exit.md` | Own your stack; avoid proprietary lock-in | Any infrastructure, contract, or vendor selection |
| 09 | `09-performance-transparency.md` | Measure and publish service performance data | Any dashboard, analytics setup, or service report |
| 10 | `10-security-and-privacy-by-design.md` | Embed security and privacy from the first sprint | Any architecture decision, data model, or integration |

Default for "building or reviewing a citizen-facing transactional service": read 01, 02, 03, 04 at minimum.
