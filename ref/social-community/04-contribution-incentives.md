# Rule 04 — Contribution Incentives: Design for the 1% Who Create, Sustain the 99% Who Consume

> 기여하는 소수를 키워야 소비하는 다수가 머문다. 창작자 경험이 커뮤니티의 심장이다.
> The consuming majority stays only when the contributing minority thrives. Creator experience is the community's heartbeat.

## Why

Every large UGC platform follows a contribution inequality: roughly 1% of users create the majority of content, 9% comment or react, and 90% consume silently. Kraut and Resnick's evidence-based framework shows that commitment and contribution are not defaults — they must be designed through selective incentives, recognition, and reduced friction. If the posting experience is painful or rewards are invisible, the 1% churn, content volume collapses, and the 90% have nothing to consume. Contribution incentives are not gamification decoration; they are the supply-side engine of the entire platform.

## How to apply

- Measure the contributor funnel separately from the consumer funnel: track impression-to-post rate, post-to-comment rate, and first-post-to-second-post rate as distinct KPIs.
- Reward early contribution explicitly: surface new posts to a wider audience than their author's follower count warrants, so first-time contributors see real engagement.
- Implement lightweight reputation signals (upvotes, karma, badges, contributor levels) that are visible on profiles; these function as social proof and spur continued contribution.
- Reduce the marginal cost of contributing: rich-text composer, drag-and-drop media, saved drafts, mobile-first posting, and cross-posting to sub-communities must all be fast and low-friction.
- Notify contributors of meaningful engagement (first comment, first upvote, reply from a high-rep user) within minutes; delayed gratification breaks the contribution loop.

## Checklist

- [ ] Contributor funnel metrics are instrumented and reviewed separately from consumer metrics.
- [ ] New posts from low-follower accounts receive algorithmic distribution uplift for a defined window.
- [ ] Reputation or recognition system exists and is visible on public profiles.
- [ ] Post composer is tested on mobile and completes the creation flow in under 60 seconds for a text post.
- [ ] First-engagement notifications (comment, upvote, reply) fire within 5 minutes of the triggering event.
