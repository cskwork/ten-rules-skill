# Rule 10 — Measure, Monitor, and Iterate

> 측정하지 않으면 SEO가 아니라 추측이다.
> Without measurement it is not SEO, it is guessing.

## Why

SEO is a continuous loop, not a one-time setup: algorithms update, competitors move, and pages decay. Google Search Console reports the queries, impressions, clicks, coverage, and experience data that ground decisions in evidence rather than opinion. Without measurement you cannot tell a winning change from a losing one, or detect a regression before it costs traffic. Verify outcomes; do not assume a change worked.

## How to apply

- Connect Google Search Console (and analytics) before making changes, to establish a baseline.
- Track queries, impressions, clicks, and average position for target pages.
- Monitor index coverage, page experience, and structured-data reports for regressions.
- Change one thing at a time where possible, and compare against the baseline.
- Refresh decaying content and revisit intent as results and competitors shift.

## Checklist

- [ ] Search Console and analytics are connected with a recorded baseline
- [ ] Target queries, clicks, and positions are tracked over time
- [ ] Coverage, experience, and structured-data reports are monitored for errors
- [ ] Changes are evaluated against the baseline, not assumed effective
- [ ] Decaying pages are identified and refreshed on a schedule
