# Rule 04 — Demonstrate E-E-A-T and Trust

> 신뢰는 글이 아니라 증거로 증명한다.
> Trust is proven by evidence, not by assertion.

## Why

The Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines evaluate pages by E-E-A-T — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust — with Trust at the center. Raters do not set rankings directly, but they calibrate the systems that do, so E-E-A-T describes what those systems aim to reward. It is decisive for YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) topics, where weak credibility is actively suppressed. Demonstrate first-hand experience and verifiable expertise; never just claim them.

## How to apply

- Attribute content to a named author with relevant, verifiable credentials and a bio.
- Show first-hand experience: original photos, testing, results, or lived knowledge.
- Cite reputable primary sources and link to them.
- Make trust signals visible: contact details, ownership, policies, and accurate dates.
- Apply the highest scrutiny to YMYL topics (health, finance, safety, legal, civics).

## Checklist

- [ ] Content is attributed to a qualified, identifiable author
- [ ] First-hand experience is shown, not merely asserted
- [ ] Claims cite reputable primary sources
- [ ] Contact, ownership, and policy pages are present and accurate
- [ ] YMYL content meets a higher bar for sourcing and accuracy
