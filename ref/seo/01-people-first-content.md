# Rule 01 — Create Helpful, People-First Content

> 검색엔진이 아니라 사람을 위해 콘텐츠를 만들어라.
> Make content for people, not for search engines.

## Why

Google's foundational guidance is to create helpful, reliable, people-first content; its ranking systems reward content that satisfies a real person, not content engineered solely to rank. Content built primarily for engines is the defining trait Google's helpful content guidance warns against. Original perspective — case studies, first-hand examples, and analysis — outranks generic, derivative answers. This is the principle every other SEO rule serves.

## How to apply

- Start from a real audience question and a goal: what should the reader be able to do after reading.
- Add original value: data, examples, testing, or a viewpoint not already on page one.
- Answer the question fully so the reader does not need to search again.
- Disclose how content was produced, including any AI assistance, and keep a human accountable for accuracy.
- Cut filler, padding, and keyword-driven sections that serve ranking but not readers.

## Checklist

- [ ] The page answers a specific question the audience actually asks
- [ ] It contains original insight, data, or experience not found verbatim elsewhere
- [ ] A reader leaves satisfied without needing to search again
- [ ] No section exists only to capture a keyword
- [ ] Authorship and production method are clear and accountable
