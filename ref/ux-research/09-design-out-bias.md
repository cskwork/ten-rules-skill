# Rule 09 — Design Out Bias

> 편향은 기본값이다. 설계와 분석 단계마다 제거하라.
> Bias is the default; engineer it out at every step of design and analysis.

## Why

Erika Hall's Just Enough Research stresses that the biggest threat to good research is the researcher's own blind spots and biases. Confirmation bias makes us hear what we expected; recency and primacy distort recall; sampling bias skews who we hear from. Nielsen Norman Group catalogs facilitation biases that contaminate sessions in real time. Bias is not an accident to apologize for later; it is the baseline state to actively counter.

## How to apply

- State your hypotheses and hopes up front, then watch for evidence that contradicts them.
- Standardize scripts, tasks, and order across participants to limit facilitator drift.
- Separate observation from interpretation; record what happened before deciding what it means.
- Have a second person review notes and themes to check for confirmation bias.
- Watch for sampling, leading, social-desirability, and recency biases by name during synthesis.

## Checklist

- [ ] Hypotheses were written before data collection and tested against contradicting evidence
- [ ] Tasks, script, and order were consistent across participants
- [ ] Observation is recorded separately from interpretation
- [ ] A second reviewer checked themes for confirmation bias
