# UX Research — 10 Rules (index)

Source: market-researched synthesis grounded in Nielsen Norman Group (Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics, "5 users"), Steve Krug (Don't Make Me Think), Erika Hall (Just Enough Research), and ISO 9241-11. UX Research is the disciplined study of real users' behavior, needs, and context to reduce risk and guide product decisions.

Pick the rules that bear on the task, then open those files for the full Why / How / Checklist.

| # | File | Principle | Read when the task touches |
|---|------|-----------|----------------------------|
| 01 | `01-define-question-before-method.md` | Frame the decision and research question before picking a method | study planning, scoping, kickoffs |
| 02 | `02-observe-behavior-not-opinions.md` | Trust what users do over what they say | interviews, tests, surveys, claims |
| 03 | `03-test-early-and-often.md` | Small, frequent, iterative tests beat one big study | when to test, cadence, budget |
| 04 | `04-match-method-to-question.md` | Qualitative answers why; quantitative answers how many | choosing a method, mixed methods |
| 05 | `05-ask-without-leading.md` | Neutral, open-ended questions; never prime the answer | scripts, moderation, interviews |
| 06 | `06-right-size-the-sample.md` | Sample size follows method and goal, not habit | recruiting numbers, significance |
| 07 | `07-recruit-the-right-users.md` | Findings only generalize to who you actually studied | screeners, recruiting, panels |
| 08 | `08-evaluate-against-heuristics.md` | Inspect against Nielsen's 10 heuristics before/with testing | usability review, audits, no-budget |
| 09 | `09-design-out-bias.md` | Bias is the default; engineer it out at every step | analysis, study design, synthesis |
| 10 | `10-turn-findings-into-decisions.md` | Research that does not change a decision is waste | reporting, prioritization, handoff |

Default for "running a usability study to improve a product": read 01, 02, 03, and 10 at minimum.
