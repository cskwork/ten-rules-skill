# Rule 05 — Ask Without Leading

> 답을 심지 마라. 열린 질문을 던지고 중립을 지켜라.
> Never plant the answer: ask open-ended questions and stay neutral.

## Why

Leading questions produce biased or false answers because participants mimic the interviewer's wording, agenda, or hopes. Nielsen Norman Group documents this as a top facilitation failure and recommends open-ended questions plus the funnel technique (broad before specific). Most leading is unintentional, so neutral phrasing and body language must be engineered, not assumed.

## How to apply

- Phrase questions open-ended ("Tell me about...") rather than yes/no or multiple-choice when seeking insight.
- Use the funnel technique: start broad, then narrow to specific follow-ups.
- Strip assumptions, value words, and the desired answer out of every question ("How easy was it?" presumes easy).
- Stay neutral in tone and body language; do not nod approval or react to "wrong" answers.
- Let silence work; do not rescue a pause by suggesting an answer.

## Checklist

- [ ] No question embeds the expected or hoped-for answer
- [ ] Core questions are open-ended, not closed
- [ ] Script moves broad-to-narrow (funnel), not narrow-first
- [ ] Facilitator reactions stay neutral across all participants
