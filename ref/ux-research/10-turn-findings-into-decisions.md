# Rule 10 — Turn Findings Into Decisions

> 결정을 바꾸지 못하는 연구는 낭비다. 발견을 우선순위 행동으로 옮겨라.
> Research that changes no decision is waste; convert findings into prioritized action.

## Why

Erika Hall's Just Enough Research is built on the premise that research exists to inform decisions, not to produce reports. Steve Krug's Don't Make Me Think pushes teams to fix the most serious problems first and ship, rather than debate. Nielsen Norman Group recommends severity ratings precisely so findings rank into an action queue. A study that ends in a deck nobody acts on has failed regardless of rigor.

## How to apply

- Rank each finding by severity and frequency, and map it to a concrete recommendation.
- Tie every key finding back to the decision named in Rule 01.
- Fix the most serious problems first; do not let minor polish outrank blockers.
- Report so a busy stakeholder can act in minutes: top issues, evidence, recommended action.
- Close the loop by tracking which findings led to changes, and re-test them.

## Checklist

- [ ] Each finding has a severity and a specific recommended action
- [ ] Findings map back to the original decision and owner
- [ ] The most severe problems are prioritized above cosmetic ones
- [ ] A mechanism tracks which findings were acted on and re-tested
