# Rule 07 — Recruit the Right Users

> 누구를 연구했는지가 곧 결과의 범위다. 진짜 사용자를 모집하라.
> Findings generalize only to whom you actually studied; recruit real, representative users.

## Why

ISO 9241-11 ties usability to specified users in a specified context, so testing the wrong people gives precisely wrong answers. The 5-user rule from Nielsen Norman Group only holds when participants represent a relatively homogeneous target group; mismatched recruits produce noise that looks like insight. Convenience samples (colleagues, friends, whoever is nearby) are a common and costly trap.

## How to apply

- Write a screener that selects on real behaviors and context, not demographics alone.
- Recruit people who do the task today, not those who merely resemble them.
- Exclude internal staff and people who know the product or the team.
- Segment when groups behave differently, and cover each segment.
- Document who was studied so readers know the boundary of the findings.

## Checklist

- [ ] Screener selects on relevant behavior/context, not just demographics
- [ ] No colleagues, friends, or product-insiders in the sample
- [ ] Each distinct user segment is represented
- [ ] The report states who was studied and the resulting scope limit
