# Rule 04 — Match the Method to the Question

> 질문에 맞는 방법을 골라라. "왜"는 정성, "얼마나"는 정량이다.
> Pick the method the question demands: qualitative answers why, quantitative answers how many.

## Why

Qualitative methods (interviews, observation, think-aloud tests) explain why behavior happens and uncover unknown problems; quantitative methods (surveys, analytics, A/B tests, benchmarks) measure how widespread or large an effect is. Nielsen Norman Group draws the line clearly: a qualitative study with 5 users diagnoses issues, while quantitative claims need statistical power. Using the wrong type yields confident but invalid answers.

## How to apply

- Use qualitative methods when you need to discover problems or understand reasons.
- Use quantitative methods when you need to size, rank, or prove an effect with confidence intervals.
- Pair them deliberately: qualitative to generate hypotheses, quantitative to confirm at scale.
- Choose attitudinal methods for what users think, behavioral methods for what they do.
- Do not report percentages or significance from a handful of qualitative sessions.

## Checklist

- [ ] Method type (qual/quant) matches whether the question is "why" or "how many"
- [ ] No statistical claims are drawn from small qualitative samples
- [ ] Mixed-method studies state which method does discovery vs confirmation
- [ ] Attitudinal vs behavioral choice fits the question
